Machwake

Machine speed. Human accountability.

The open collaboration layer for accountable human‑agent engineering teams.

No agent without a principal.

No run without a pactum.

No change without a wake.

Pactum

Defines who an agent acts for, its mandate, its authority, and where approval stays with a person.

Wake

Preserves the verifiable trail from conversation and decision through run, code, tests, commit and review.

Open source · Early prototype
